WordPress Theme and Plugin

For my WordPress website, I chose the Astra theme because it has a simple and clean design. It also gives me options to customize the layout and appearance of my website. I selected WPForms Lite plugin because it allows me to add contact forms to my website. The theme improves the appearance of my website, while the plugin adds features that make it easier for visitors to communicate. Together, they make my website more useful and professional.
